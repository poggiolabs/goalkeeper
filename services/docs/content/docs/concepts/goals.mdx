---
title: Goals
description: Outcomes that guide work performed by AI agents.
---

A goal describes an outcome for one or more AI agents to pursue. It provides a
shared definition of success while leaving execution details to the agents
responsible for the work.

Goals should be specific enough to evaluate, durable across individual agent
runs, and explicit about any constraints that affect completion.

Each goal belongs to an organization and records a short title, a durable
prompt, an accountable organization member, an optional measurement method,
and a lifecycle status: `active`, `paused`, `completed`, or `archived`.
Organization-defined labels provide stable taxonomy and can be renamed without
changing goal references.

Schedules and execution runs are intentionally outside the goal state model.
They can be introduced as separate domains if their lifecycle and ownership
requirements become clear.
